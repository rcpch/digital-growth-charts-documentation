# Clinical Safety

The RCPCH Digital Growth Charts platform assists suitably qualified healthcare professionals by calculating and presenting paediatric growth parameters relative to recognised growth references. It supports, but does not replace, clinical assessment, interpretation, or decision-making, and it does not provide a diagnosis.

The intended users, patient population, use environment, limitations, and warranted service are defined in the canonical [Intended Purpose](docs/safety/csmf/intended-purpose.md). The platform is registered with the MHRA as a Class I medical device; the current registration record and its limitations are documented in [MHRA Registration](docs/safety/medical-device-reg/mhra.md).

## Safety Status And Ownership

The Clinical Safety Management File is maintained in this repository under the responsibility of the named Clinical Safety Officer. Current roles and document-control responsibilities are recorded in the [Clinical Risk Management Plan](docs/safety/csmf/clinical-risk-mgmt-plan.md) and [Quality Management System](docs/safety/qms.md).

Safety and regulatory documents are controlled material. Changes require the review defined by `.github/CODEOWNERS` and must preserve the associated evidence trail.
